01 · Summary, in plain language.

IQS Flow is a quality control platform for cleaning and facility service companies and the facilities they serve. It is made by Integrity Quality Solutions.

  • We collect what the product needs to show who did what work, where and when: account details, work records, photos, and the location of your phone when you use the app for work.
  • While the app is open, IQS Flow records your location when you clock in, start or finish work, submit a checklist, inspection or issue report, use the map, or look up the area you are in. For cleaners, during a scheduled shift and not on a break, it also checks in about every 3 minutes. It never collects location when the app is closed or in the background.
  • Supervisors and managers at your employer can see your location. Coworkers who use the app can see your name and your most recent location from today on the app’s map. Your employer’s clients can see where each inspection they view was submitted, and the live position of workers at their own sites from the last 30 minutes, with each worker’s name unless your employer hides names.
  • Inspection photos are analyzed by Google’s Gemini AI to suggest a cleanliness score. People make the final call.
  • We do not sell personal information. We do not use advertising or tracking tools.
  • Each customer’s data is kept separate from every other customer’s data.
  • You can ask us to delete your account in the app (More, then Delete my account) or by email. We complete deletion within 30 days and tell you when it is done. TODO(Josh): do not publish this promise until the deletion process in section 09 exists.

Work records

  • Photos you take or choose as evidence, for example for an inspection, a checklist, a work order, or an issue report.
  • Messages you send to coworkers in the app, compliments, and suggestions you make, such as a task that is missing from a checklist.
  • Support requests you send us, including any screenshots you attach.

Location

  • The location of your phone when you use the app for work: when you clock in, start or finish a task or work order, submit a checklist, inspection or issue report, use the map, or look up the area you are in. For cleaners, the app also checks in about every 3 minutes during a scheduled shift. Section 04 explains exactly when.
  • The location saved in a photo, if your phone’s camera saves one (see section 04).

Device and usage information

  • The app version and update version, your phone’s operating system and version, your language setting, a push notification token, and a random installation ID that the app’s update system creates on your phone.
  • Crash reports from the app. These include error details and your user ID so we can find and fix the problem.
  • How features are used, for example which suggestion you pick in search, so we can make those features better.
  • Standard server logs kept by our hosting provider, such as IP address, time and the address requested, for security and troubleshooting.

What the app does not do

  • It does not record audio. Read-aloud uses your phone’s built-in text-to-speech. If you use voice typing, that is a feature of your phone’s keyboard, and the keyboard provider’s privacy policy applies.
  • It does not show ads, and it does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • It does not read your contacts or calendar.

What is stored on your phone

  • Your IQS Flow sign-in session, kept in your phone’s secure storage. If you sign in with an email address or a Google account, Google Firebase Authentication also keeps its own sign-in session in the app’s storage.
  • Work you do while offline, including photos, until it uploads. A list of the photos waiting to upload is kept in secure storage.
  • Saved translations and your settings.

Deleting the app removes most of this from your phone, including any work that has not uploaded yet. On iPhone, what is in secure storage (the iOS Keychain) can remain after you delete the app: your IQS Flow sign-in session, until it expires, and the list of photos that were waiting to upload. Signing out before you delete the app removes the sign-in session. Deleting the app does not delete your account. To delete your account, see section 09.

App updates and notifications

The app gets updates through Expo, our app update provider. When the app checks for an update, Expo receives the app version, the platform, your IP address and the app’s random installation ID. Push notifications are delivered through Expo’s push service, which passes them to Apple (on iPhone) or Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (on Android).

05 · AI features.

IQS Flow uses Google’s Gemini AI models, through the Gemini API, for the features below. Photo scoring is on for all customer accounts.

  • Photo scoring. When an evidence photo for an inspection is uploaded, and again when the inspection is submitted, we send the inspection’s evidence photos to Gemini with a fixed instruction. Gemini suggests a cleanliness score from 0 to 100 and a one-sentence reason. We do not send your name with the photo. A photo file can include a location saved by your phone’s camera (section 04).
  • The score is only a suggestion. It is shown to the people who review the inspection. It never passes or fails an inspection on its own.
  • We keep the suggestion next to the human result so we can measure how accurate it is and improve our scoring. TODO(Josh): with counsel, say whether IQS will train its own model on these photos and scores (the code already stores each photo with its human and AI scores as a training sample), and whether customers can opt out. See the processor question in section 02.
  • Text features. Gemini also helps with search suggestions, the in-app help chat, building checklists and forms, matching forms to compliance standards, and written summaries in reports. For these we send the text you type and the related site, checklist or report details.
  • Client portal assistant. People at a client organization can ask the assistant in the client portal about their facilities. With each question we send Gemini a summary of that organization’s data: its vendors and their worker counts, its sites and compliance scores, the titles of open issues, its compliance programs, vendor spend and upcoming payments.
  • Photos can show people. When you take evidence photos, please keep people out of the frame where you can.
