01 · Summary, in plain language.

IQS Flow is a quality control platform for cleaning and facility service companies and the facilities they serve. It is made by Integrity Quality Solutions.

  • We collect what the product needs to show who did what work, where and when: account details, work records, photos, and the location of your phone when you use the app for work.
  • While the app is open, IQS Flow records your location when you clock in, start or finish work, submit a checklist, inspection or issue report, use the map, or look up the area you are in. For cleaners, during a scheduled shift and not on a break, it also checks in about every 3 minutes. It never collects location when the app is closed or in the background.
  • Supervisors and managers at your employer can see your location. Coworkers who use the app can see your name and your most recent location from today on the app’s map. Your employer’s clients can see where each inspection they view was submitted.
  • Inspection photos are analyzed by Google’s Gemini AI to suggest a cleanliness score. People make the final call.
  • We do not sell personal information. We do not use advertising or tracking tools.
  • Each customer’s data is kept separate from every other customer’s data.
  • You can ask us to delete your account in the app (More, then Delete my account) or by email. We complete deletion within 30 days and tell you when it is done. TODO(Josh): do not publish this promise until the deletion process in section 09 exists.

If your organization has a signed agreement with us, that agreement controls how we handle your organization’s data where it differs from this policy.

03 · What we collect.

Account information

  • Your name, role, the organization you belong to, and the sites and areas you are assigned to.
  • Your email address and phone number, if your employer adds them. Many cleaners do not have an email address in IQS Flow.
  • Sign-in details. Cleaners sign in with their first and last name and a 4-digit code from their employer. Your employer may set this code to the last four digits of your Social Security number. We store only a scrambled, one-way version of the code (a bcrypt hash), never the digits themselves. Staff who sign in with an email address or a Google account do so through Google Firebase Authentication.
  • Your language and notification settings.

Work records

  • Shifts, clock-in and clock-out times, tasks, checklists, inspections, scores, work orders, notes, and the time of each action.
  • Photos you take or choose as evidence, for example for an inspection, a checklist, a work order, or an issue report.
  • Messages you send to coworkers in the app, compliments, and suggestions you make, such as a task that is missing from a checklist.
  • Support requests you send us, including any screenshots you attach.

Location

  • The location of your phone when you use the app for work: when you clock in, start or finish a task or work order, submit a checklist, inspection or issue report, use the map, or look up the area you are in. For cleaners, the app also checks in about every 3 minutes during a scheduled shift. Section 04 explains exactly when.

Device and usage information

  • The app version and update version, your phone’s operating system and version, your language setting, and a push notification token.
  • Crash reports from the app. These include error details and your user ID so we can find and fix the problem.
  • How features are used, for example which suggestion you pick in search, so we can make those features better.
  • Standard server logs kept by our hosting provider, such as IP address, time and the address requested, for security and troubleshooting.

Website visitors and people who contact us

  • When you fill in a form on iqsflow.com (contact, newsletter or job application), we receive what you enter, such as your name, email address, organization, message, and any resume or profile links. Form submissions are emailed to our team. We do not store them in a database.
  • Our website forms use Google reCAPTCHA to block spam. reCAPTCHA collects device and browser information, and Google’s Privacy Policy and Terms of Service apply to it.
  • Our website loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google when you visit.
  • We do not use advertising or analytics cookies on iqsflow.com or in the IQS Flow dashboard. The dashboard uses a sign-in cookie to keep you signed in.

What is stored on your phone

  • Your sign-in session, kept in your phone’s secure storage.
  • Work you do while offline, including photos, until it uploads.

Deleting the app removes these from your phone, including any work that has not uploaded yet. Deleting the app does not delete your account. To delete your account, see section 09.

App updates and notifications

The app gets updates through Expo, our app update provider. When the app checks for an update, Expo receives the app version, the platform and your IP address. Push notifications are delivered through Expo’s push service, which passes them to Apple (on iPhone) or Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (on Android).

05 · AI features.

IQS Flow uses Google’s Gemini AI models, through the Gemini API, for the features below. Photo scoring is on for all customer accounts.

  • Photo scoring. When an inspection is submitted, we send its evidence photos (one photo per checklist item) to Gemini with a fixed instruction. Gemini suggests a cleanliness score from 0 to 100 and a one-sentence reason. We do not send your name with the photo.
  • The score is only a suggestion. It is shown to the people who review the inspection. It never passes or fails an inspection on its own.
  • We keep the suggestion next to the human result so we can measure how accurate it is and improve our scoring.   • Text features. Gemini also helps with search suggestions, the in-app help chat, building checklists and forms, matching forms to compliance standards, and written summaries in reports. For these we send the text you type and the related site, checklist or report details.
  • Photos can show people. When you take evidence photos, please keep people out of the frame where you can.

11 · Security.

  • Connections to IQS Flow are encrypted (HTTPS).

  • Passwords and sign-in codes are stored only as one-way hashes.
  • Access is limited by role, and each customer’s data is kept separate.
  • Changes to important records are kept in a tamper-evident audit log (see section 08).
