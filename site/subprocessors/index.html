06 · Services that receive no personal data from us.

  • AeroDataBox (through RapidAPI): flight schedules for airport sites. We send only airport codes and time ranges.
  • OpenStreetMap tile servers, or CARTO when enabled: map images in the web dashboard. Your browser loads the images directly, so the provider sees your IP address and the map area on screen. We do not send them names or work records.
  • unpkg and cdnjs: map pin images in the web dashboard. Your browser loads them directly, so the provider sees your IP address.
