Installing on Android

A one-time, two-minute setup. Updates after this arrive automatically inside the app.

1
Tap Download for Android above

Your browser downloads a file called iqs-flow.apk.

2
Open the downloaded file

Find it in your notifications or the Downloads folder, then tap it.

3
Allow installs from this source

If Android says it's "not allowed to install unknown apps from this source," tap Settings on that message and turn on Allow from this source. This is a normal one-time step for apps installed outside the Play Store, and it is safe for IQS Flow.

4
Tap Install, then Open

Look for the IQS Flow icon on your home screen.

Signing in: cleaning staff tap "I'm a cleaner" and enter their first name, last name, and the last 4 digits of their SSN — no email needed. Inspection staff and managers use the Email tab with their work email and password.
